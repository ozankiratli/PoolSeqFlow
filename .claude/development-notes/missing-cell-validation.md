# Missing-cell scenarios in the validation harness

**Written 2026-10-06, against `dev` at `7419281` plus uncommitted work**: the association fix for unread units (`association-unread-units.md`), the depth-mask redesign (`depth-masking-proposal.md`), and this change. Every number below was read from the harness as committed in this change, at its default seed 20260907 and default replicate count, under R 4.6.1.

## What was asked

Z decided on 2026-10-05 that missing-cell scenarios are to be added to the validation harness, alongside `vcffilter.keepLowDepthAsZero`. The proposal note had named the gap: nothing measured what masking does to association's calibration or to where mds places a shallow pool, and nothing tested whether missingness is informative. Z opened the work on 2026-10-06: "Let's move with the next task. missing cell scenarios?"

## How it was designed

A workflow, `wf_831b725c-90d` (transcript under `~/.claude/projects/-home-tholian-Nextcloud-GitHub-PoolSeqFlow/7de5ecd1-0266-4a1d-aeb2-69c4394fc0c5/subagents/workflows/`), every agent on Sonnet 5.5: three independent proposals, one each on calibration, on how cells really go missing in pool-seq, and on building it in this harness, then a synthesis that resolved their conflicts and wrote and ran a prototype of every piece. It took 150 minutes and 424 tool calls, far longer than expected, because the proposal agents ran the measurements themselves; Z chose to let it finish. The prototypes were then ported into the harness in its own style with the order of every random draw kept, and the port reproduces every number the prototypes printed at the default seed.

The synthesis resolved four conflicts that are worth keeping. The estimator calls the module's own functions rather than a restatement of them, with a brute-force oracle as the independent check. Read sets are enumerated, every pattern of read units in every cohort, wherever a deterministic check or an exact stratum is wanted, because the module groups sites by which units were read and a grouping bug shows only on the patterns it gets wrong; realistic compositions are drawn only where the composition is itself the quantity. Rates over enumerated p are gated on four standard errors of the larger of the binomial and the across-cohort spread, and rates over shared nuisance on a t quantile of the spread alone, after a Monte Carlo of 400,000 nulls showed that four standard errors of a spread from four to eight cohorts alarm 0.2 to 1.1 percent per comparison. And OFF, step 7's default rule, is read as the six-unit stratum of the masked table for per-site rates, and run as a table of its own only where table-level numbers are compared.

## What the sections measured

**`missing_null`: the masked p is the exact m-unit test.** Over every pattern of read units in seven cells, 18,605 tested sites, no p fell below 1/m!, every n_observed was the units the generator read, every site that must carry no statistic carried none, and every p equaled a brute-force enumeration of the m! orders to 1e-9 (largest gap 0). The rejected scheme, unread units zeroed under one rearrangement of all six, put 47 of 767 partly read sites below 1/m!, disagreed with the oracle at 98 percent of them, and rejected 0.077 of the three-unit sites against a bound of 0, so the gates can see it. Pooled rates at four, five and six units read 0.0319, 0.0471 and 0.0493 against bounds of 0.0417, 0.05 and 0.05. At resolution, 8,000 sites per stratum, flat depth read 0.0286, 0.0524 and 0.0494, the last two the two-sided control, and aligned depth 0.0366, 0.0501 and 0.0527. Four units sit 20 to 30 percent under their bound from count ties alone: any tie among four frequencies makes 1/24 unreachable. The sampled path at eight units took 499 rearrangements, its smallest p was 1/(1+B), and its p less the exact p less (1 - p)/(1 + B) averaged -0.0011 against a margin of 0.0026. Units of two lanes of one pool, 57 percent of them read through one lane, kept their n_observed and read 0.0363, 0.0466 and 0.0514.

**`missing_published`: what a run says about itself.** Masking kept 99 percent of raw sites where the default rule kept 38, 12 and 0.7 percent at 15 percent, 30 percent and a ladder of 94 to 7 percent of cells under the floor; 0.5, 6 and 12 percent of the masked rule's sites were read in fewer than three units and untested. `lambda_gc` on a perfectly null table read 1.296, 1.485 and 1.657, each within 2 percent of the null of its own mix of units (ratios 0.994, 1.019, 0.995, and 0.993 on the aligned ladder). The null itself is 2.198, 1.465, 1.286 and 1.206 for sites read in three to six units, and 3.81 at three alleles and 6.03 at four over six units, so the published value is not near 1 on a clean table and crosses 3 on an exactly calibrated one. Theta, each cohort analyzed fully read and masked, moved by 2.1 percent of its true value at most under random, depth-floor and phenotype-aligned masks; it stayed finite with a library unread at every site and with 85 percent of cells masked, and fell 4 percent under allele-linked loss, which is reported only. With missingness lined up with the phenotype at equal depth, `depth_phenotype_cor` is NA: every unit carries the same weight, and the published diagnostic cannot see it.

**`missing_informative`: allele-linked read loss.** The ALT-carrying reads were kept with probability r, pools' true frequencies spread by F, coverage negative binomial. With depth graded 22 to 77 in phenotype order and a floor of 20, the closed-form rate at 0.05 under the default rule read 0.0557, 0.0626 and 0.0733 at r 0.4 and F 0.1, 0.3 and 0.5, against an unfloored 0.0483, 0.0486 and 0.0504; at r 0.7 and at r 1 it did not move. The same floor applied to the coverage before the loss, which no real run can do, never moved the rate by more than 0.0008, so the floor acting on a depth that depends on the allele is the mechanism. One shallow pool at one end was never inflated, only conservative. More variable coverage, size 10 against 50, roughly halved the excess. Through the module at r 0.4 and F 0.3, a site read in every unit read 0.0702 under either rule, sites read in five and four units under the mask 0.0567 and 0.0442, and the excess false calls per 1,000 raw sites were 2.88 under the default rule and 6.80 under the mask, which kept every raw site where the default kept 14 percent. The module published `depth_phenotype_cor` 0.993 and `lambda_gc` 1.43 for that table, and `depth_phenotype_cor` reads 0.97 on the harmless r = 1 ladder too. By the frequency of the allele that loses reads, the default rule read 0.0399, 0.0554, 0.0997 and 0.1647 in four bins from 0.05 to 0.95, the last two holding 10 percent of its sites.

**`missing_yield`: what masking buys.** With 10 percent of 2,000 sites planted at a slope of 0.14 and cells masked independently, the default rule kept 560 sites and found 20 percent of the planted ones; the mask kept 2,000 and found 52 percent, at null rates of 0.0439 and 0.0430. When one gamma factor made the same sites shallow in every pool the gain fell to 26 against 40 percent. Under allele-linked loss the unfloored table found 72 percent, the default rule 18 at a null rate of 0.0629, and the mask 49. `minSamples` 2 and 3 found the same sites, 4 lost none, and 5 lost 13 percent. BH selected nothing in any arm: at six units the smallest p is 1/720, so BH needs one site at that floor for every 36 tested, and these tables held 2.8 to 15 against 9 to 56 needed.

**`missing_mds`: pairwise deletion.** Over 40 studies of two clades by drift, with one pool 85 percent masked, no pair's corrected distance was biased beyond its margin under either rule (largest z 2.70 of 30 comparisons), and the mask cut the error of the pairs that do not involve the shallow pool 3.3 to 4.6 times against the default rule. The error of one distance went as 0.048 over the root of the sites it rests on, at a distance near 0.018: 133 percent of the distance at 4 shared sites, 61 percent at 17, 34 percent at 58 and 20 percent at 172. That is what the warning at 30 rests on, and the sites here are independent, so linked real data are noisier. With every pool one population, a pool 94 percent masked was the outermost point in the plot in 50 percent of studies under the mask and 46 percent unfloored, against 1/6 if nothing singled it out: noise, not bias. Allele-linked loss at r 0.4 shrank a divergent pool's distances to 0.35 of the truth under the default rule and 0.49 under the mask, and turned a star into a depth gradient in the plot (Spearman of depth with distance from the center 0.68 under the mask).

## Bite proofs

Every gate was run against a mutant meant to break it, from scratch copies and in-memory parses, never the repository. In `missing_null`: grouping sites by how many units were read instead of which failed H4 at 4,942 sites; a +1 on the enumerated p failed H4 at 8,197; `roll_up()` without `na.rm` passed the patterns, which hold one pool per unit, and failed the lanes arm at 4,885 sites; an absolute tie tolerance failed H1 and H4 at 10 sites, all in the near-collinear cell; the unmutated module passed both. A dispersion count of one instead of two cannot be caught by any table and is not a gap. In `agree.R`: three one-line mutants of the main loop -- untestable below two units instead of three, BH over every site instead of the tested, theta never applied to the weights -- each disagreed, at 20, 4 and 10 comparisons; the unmutated copy agreed. Through the suite's own case, from a stand-in `REPO_ROOT`, the theta mutant failed it and the unmutated tree passed. The lanes mutant first stopped the section with an R error rather than a FAIL, because the port had dropped an `isTRUE` the prototype carried; every rate gate now has one.

## A correction to an earlier note

`association-unread-units.md` says a sampled p "is an unbiased estimate" of the m-unit test. It is valid and not unbiased: with reached ~ Binomial(B, p), the mean of (1 + reached)/(1 + B) is p + (1 - p)/(1 + B). The sampled arm of `missing_null` subtracts exactly that and gates what is left at zero.

## Where the read loss happens, ruled by Z the same day

Asked when reads carrying one allele get lost, the answer was: alignment scores them (`bwa mem -T 30`), and the filters after it remove them -- the BAM cleanup's `samtools view -q 30 -f 0x2 -F 0xF0C`, and at variant calling `bcftools mpileup -C 100 -q 30 -Q 30`, whose survivors are what `FORMAT/DP` counts -- with a deletion haplotype contributing no reads at all. Z: "All of those are correct calls. Nothing to change." The reason Z gave: the pipeline promises the user a very conservative number, so every read it counts must be of high quality, which is why only properly paired reads are kept. The manual already says so, in the cleanup walkthrough ("deliberately strict") and in the account of the design. The filters stay as they are, and allele-linked loss is not a reason to revisit them: it is the price of that promise, paid in depth. A check offered at the same time, a pool's `FORMAT/DP` against its non-reference frequency in the step-6 call set, was not taken up.

## The mds warning at 30 shared sites, built the same day

`mds` `20261004.001` became `20261006.001`. A pair averaged over fewer than 30 sites is flagged in three places: `few_sites` in `distance.tsv`, 1 below 30; a line on the run's console, under the target's name, naming up to ten such pairs; and a count under `mds.png`. There is no setting for it, because a warning changes no number. The same version drops a clause from a manifest gate and from a comment in `main.nf` that said a cell with no reads is published as 0 in the frequency table; it has been NA since `772db1f`.

**Why stdout, and why `debug true` on the module's process.** Measured with a two-process Nextflow script: a task that succeeds shows nothing on the console unless its process sets `debug`, and then only its stdout, never its stderr. So `mds.R` writes the warning with `cat()` and `main.nf` prints it, prefixed `MDS <label>:`. Rendered through the frame on the corpus, whose fourteen sites put every pair under the line, the run printed "MDS Output: 15 of 15 pairs of pools rest on fewer than 30 shared sites" and the pairs, and the plot carried the count.

**Found on the way, not fixed:** association's warnings are `message()` calls, to stderr, and its process sets no `debug`, so in a run through the frame none of them reaches the console -- the ranking sentence at three units, the count of tested sites that cannot reach 0.05, "no site was tested", and the note that ggplot2 is missing. They are seen only by the test suite's direct runs, which capture stderr. Reported to Z.

Tested by `test_a_pair_on_few_sites_is_flagged`, forty sites with one pool emptied at eleven of them and then at ten, so the boundary is read from both sides at 29 and 30, and by a line in the frame case that the warning reaches the console. Four mutants, from stand-in copies of the repository, each failed the case meant for them: the comparison made `<=`, the flag fixed at 0, `debug true` removed, and the first line of the warning moved to stderr.

## Left for Z, not acted on

- Whether allele-linked loss under a depth floor becomes a documented limit or a guard. The published diagnostics do not separate the harmful case from its harmless twin, so a guard would need a diagnostic that does not exist yet. Two candidates were measured but not designed: the share of each unit's cells read, by phenotype, and a within-pool footprint, the correlation across sites of a pool's depth with its frequency of one allele, near 0 without loss and -0.4 to -0.8 with it, whose sign depends on which allele normalization made the reference.
- BH over the sites that can reach the threshold, instead of every tested site.
- The sampled arm runs at an explicit budget of 499, and says nothing about the module's default at eight units or more.
- `minSamples` stays at 2 on this evidence: for association 3 is identical, and 2 is what mds pairwise deletion needs.
- The generator's ranges -- coverage size, F, r, the masking ladders -- are assumptions printed in each section's header, not fitted to any library, and should be quoted as such if the manual uses them.

## Not covered

Real data, which `Project/` holds and nothing here touches. Linkage: every site is independent. The far tail below alpha 0.05. Covariates, time series and several phenotypes at once. The false-positive filter and major-allele normalization, which the generator does not simulate; a cell's depth is the sum of its counts, so cells where INFO/DP and AD differ are outside what this can say. Two module defects are routed around rather than tested: association stops on a table of one site, and enumeration is capped at eight units whatever the budget.
